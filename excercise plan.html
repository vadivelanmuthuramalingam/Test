90-Day Recovery Plan — L4–L5 & L5–S1 Disc Bulge

Includes: Day 1–7 detailed schedule, week-by-week 12-week plan (templates for daily blocks), checklist and a progress tracker table.

How to use this plan

This plan was created for your MRI findings (L5–S1 asymmetrical left paracentral bulge with annular fissure, mild nerve irritation; L4–L5 mild bulge). It emphasizes graded activity, core & glute strengthening, nerve protection, posture, and progressive sitting tolerance. Follow your physician's advice about medication. Stop or reduce activity if you develop new numbness, severe increasing leg weakness, or bowel/bladder problems and seek immediate medical care.

Quick reference - exercise legend

  • Pelvic tilt — gentle neutralizing press of lumbar to floor.
  • Cat–Cow — slow spinal flexion/extension mobility.
  • Bird-Dog — opposite arm/leg extension (core stabilizer).
  • Glute Bridge / Heel-dig Bridge — glute & hamstring activation.
  • Dead Bug — supine core stabilization (limb control).
  • Side plank / plank — progressive core endurance.
  • Hamstring stretch (seated) — gentle posterior chain flexibility.
  • QL stretch — side bending with arm overhead (gentle).
  • McKenzie extension — standing backward extension, short reps.

Full 3-Month Plan — Weekly Templates & Daily Block Patterns

Below are week-by-week templates. Each week follows a graded pattern: increased walking/cardio, progressive strengthening, and increasing sitting tolerance. Use the Day A/B/C variations to rotate intensity and avoid overuse.

Week structure (weeks 2–12)

WeekFocusDaily pattern (A / B / C)
Week 2Mobility → begin low-load strengthening A: Walk 20–25 min, Bird-dog 2×8, Heel-dig bridge 2×10; Sit tolerance 60 min total
B: Walk 20 min, Dead-bug 2×8, Hamstring curls 2×10; Sit tolerance 60–75 min
C: Walk 25 min, Pelvic tilts + QL stretch; Sit tolerance 75 min
Week 3Strength & endurance A: Walk 25–30 min, Bird-dog 3×8, Heel-dig bridge 3×10; Sit 90 min with breaks
B: Walk 25 min, Dead-bug 3×8, Glute bridge march 2×10
C: Walk 30 min, Hamstring strengthening + QL release
Week 4Increase load slightly A: Walk 30 min, Bird-dog 3×10, Plank 10–15s ×3
B: Walk 25–30 min, Dead-bug 3×10, Heel-dig bridge 3×12
C: Walk 30–35 min, Hamstring curls 3×12, QL & hip stretches
Week 5Introduce resistance (bands) & posture work A: Walk 30–35 min, Plank 15–20s, Bird-dog 3×10, Band glute bridges 3×10
B: Walk 30 min, Dead-bug 3×10, Heel-dig bridge 3×12, Standing hamstring curls 3×12
C: Walk 35–40 min, Hip mobility + QL release
Week 6Build endurance & sitting tolerance A: Walk 35–40 min, Plank 20–30s, Glute bridge march 3×10
B: Walk 30–35 min, Dead-bug 3×12, Single-leg glute bridge 2×8
C: Walk 40 min, Hamstring eccentric work 3×10
Week 7Increase strength slowly A: Walk 40 min, Plank 30s ×3, Bird-dog 3×12
B: Walk 35 min, Romanian deadlift (bodyweight/light) 3×10, Heel-dig 3×12
C: Walk 40–45 min, Mobility + QL maintenance
Week 8Functional strengthening A: Walk 40–45 min, Plank 30–40s, Side plank 15–20s ×3
B: Walk 40 min, Glute-ham chain work, Band rows 3×10
C: Walk 45 min, QL release + hamstring maintenance
Week 9Return-to-work endurance A: Walk 45 min, Plank 40–50s, Single-leg bridge 3×10
B: Walk 40–45 min, Romanian deadlift (light) 3×10, Band rows 3×12
C: Walk 50 min, Mobility and nerve glides
Week 10Increase sitting tolerance A: Walk 50 min, Core circuit 3 rounds (plank, bird-dog, side plank short)
B: Walk 45 min, Deadlift pattern (hip hinge) bodyweight → light 3×10
C: Walk 50–55 min, Hamstring eccentric + QL care
Week 11Strength & load tolerance A: Walk 50–55 min, Plank 60s total, Step-ups or light squats 3×10
B: Walk 45–50 min, Romanian deadlift light 3×10, Band rows 3×12
C: Walk 60 min, Mobility & recovery
Week 12Maintenance & long-term habits A: Walk 60 min, Core endurance set 3× (plank/bird-dog/side plank)
B: Walk 50–60 min, Functional lifting practice light (good form)
C: Walk 60 min, QL + hamstring maintenance

Use the A/B/C pattern across each week (for example Mon=A, Tue=B, Wed=C, Thu=A, Fri=B, Sat=C, Sun=active rest/walking). Adjust intensity based on pain and fatigue. If leg weakness increases or new numbness appears, stop and consult your physician.

  • By end of week 4: pain <3 most days, sitting tolerance >45–60 min
  • By end of week 8: walking >30 minutes comfortably, sitting 90–120 min

Red flags — contact doctor immediately:
  • New or increasing numbness in both legs or saddle area
  • Sudden worsening of leg weakness, foot drop
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control
